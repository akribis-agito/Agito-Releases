TrySplit(string, int, bool, out string[], out string) 

Splits a bulk reply into one answer per command, or reports why it is malformed.


The grammar: answers are separated by <code>'>'</code>; every answer is non-empty; there is one
answer per command. A halt-on-error bulk may come back shorter only when its last answer
is an error (<code>ERR n</code>, <code>BOOT ERR n</code>, or a <code>PC Suite: ERR</code> AAComm appended
because it stopped sending at that command). Anything else is malformed: a blank answer
(an empty reply included), more answers than commands, a short halt-on-error reply that
does not end in an error, and any short continue-on-error reply. A halt-on-error bulk the
controller continued past an error comes back full-length with the error inline, which is
valid.
